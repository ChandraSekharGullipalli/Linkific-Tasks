# Data Collection Plan
## Student Academic Performance Analysis

### 1. Introduction

A data collection plan defines the procedures and methods used to gather information required for research.

This project focuses on collecting student-related information to investigate the relationship between study hours, attendance percentage, and examination scores.

### 2. Research Objectives

- Collect relevant student academic information.
- Understand student study habits.
- Examine attendance patterns.
- Analyze examination performance.
- Identify relationships between study hours, attendance, and academic scores.

### 3. Types of Data Collection

**A. Primary Data**

Primary data is collected directly from participants for a specific research purpose.

Methods:
- Online surveys.
- Google Forms questionnaires.
- Structured interviews.
- Direct responses from students.

**B. Secondary Data**

Secondary data is information that has already been collected by other researchers or organizations.

Sources:
- Public educational datasets.
- Research publications.
- Government education reports.
- Educational research websites.

### 4. Target Population

The target population consists of students studying in educational institutions.

Proposed sample size: 100 students.

The sample size is a planning target and may be adjusted according to participant availability and research requirements.

### 5. Sampling Techniques

The following sampling methods will be considered:

**Simple Random Sampling:** Every eligible student has an equal chance of selection.

**Stratified Sampling:** Students are divided into groups, such as academic years or departments, and participants are selected from each group.

**Convenience Sampling:** Participants are selected based on availability and accessibility.

Preferred method: Simple random sampling, when a suitable list of eligible students is available.

### 6. Data Collection Variables

| Variable | Description | Data Type |
|---|---|---|
| Student ID | Anonymous identifier | Categorical |
| Study Hours | Daily study time | Numerical |
| Attendance | Attendance percentage | Numerical |
| Exam Score | Examination marks | Numerical |
| Study Habits | Learning routine | Categorical |

### 7. Survey Design

A structured questionnaire will be prepared using Google Forms.

Sample questions:

1. What is your average daily study time?
2. What is your attendance percentage?
3. What is your latest examination score out of 100?
4. How frequently do you revise your lessons?
5. Do you follow a regular study schedule?
6. How many hours do you sleep daily?

Questions will be clear, relevant, and easy to answer.

### 8. Data Collection Sources

Primary sources:
- Student questionnaires.
- Google Forms responses.
- Voluntary participant responses.

Secondary sources:
- Public educational datasets.
- Educational research reports.
- Published academic studies.

### 9. Data Collection Procedure

Step 1: Define the research objectives.

Step 2: Identify the target student population.

Step 3: Prepare the survey questionnaire.

Step 4: Obtain appropriate institutional permission where required.

Step 5: Inform participants about the purpose of the study.

Step 6: Obtain informed consent.

Step 7: Distribute the questionnaire.

Step 8: Collect survey responses.

Step 9: Review and validate collected information.

Step 10: Store the dataset securely for analysis.

### 10. Data Quality Assurance

The following checks will be performed:

- Identify missing values.
- Remove duplicate submissions where appropriate.
- Validate attendance percentages between 0 and 100.
- Validate examination scores between 0 and 100.
- Check study hours for reasonable values.
- Identify inconsistent responses.
- Maintain consistent data formats.

### 11. Ethical Considerations

- Participation will be voluntary.
- Obtain informed consent before collecting responses.
- Do not collect unnecessary personal information.
- Keep student identities confidential.
- Store collected information securely.
- Use data only for the stated educational research purpose.
- Allow participants to withdraw according to the stated consent process.

### 12. Data Storage and Management

Collected information will be stored in CSV or Excel format.

Proposed file structure:

- raw_data: Original collected responses.
- processed_data: Cleaned and validated data.
- documentation: Survey and collection methodology.
- analysis: Statistical analysis outputs.

### 13. Expected Outcomes

- A structured student research dataset.
- Information about study habits and attendance.
- Examination performance records.
- A validated dataset suitable for statistical analysis.
- Research-ready data for hypothesis testing.

### 14. Conclusion

The data collection plan establishes a systematic approach to gathering reliable and relevant information for the student academic performance research project.

By using structured questionnaires, appropriate sampling techniques, data validation, and ethical research practices, the project aims to create a reliable dataset for further statistical analysis.
